What Manchester sites usually ask for

What Manchester sites ask for

CSCS labour and groundworkers, CPCS excavators and telehandlers, electricians, coded welders and SMSTS supervisors. City commercial and out-of-town civils both come through the same desk.

No Manchester office

Greater Manchester is covered from Wallasey. A start the next morning is normal. A start in an hour is not — book it the working day before.

Where this page stops

Salford, Trafford, Stockport, Bolton, Wigan and Oldham are in the weekly patch. Leeds and the rest of Yorkshire are not. Ask, and we will say if we can fill it.

Checked before they reach your site

  • CSCS verified: every operative holds a valid CSCS card, or the right scheme card for their trade (ECS for electricians, CPCS or NPORS for plant).
  • Right to work checked: before anyone is placed, with documents held on file.
  • Tickets and qualifications checked: coding certificates, SMSTS/SSSTS, NEBOSH, CCNSG and other site tickets, with expiry dates tracked in PRISM.
  • Drug and alcohol testing: programme in operation.
  • IR35 compliant: across all our contractor engagements.
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified: quality management, plus Cyber Essentials for how we handle your data.
