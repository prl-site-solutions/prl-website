Our Commitment

PRL Site Solutions Ltd is committed to acting ethically and with integrity in all of our business operations and relationships. We have a zero-tolerance approach to modern slavery and human trafficking, and we are committed to implementing and enforcing effective systems and controls to ensure these practices have no place in our business or supply chain.

This statement is made in the spirit of the Modern Slavery Act 2015 and sets out the steps we have taken to identify, assess, and address the risk of modern slavery across our operations.

About PRL Site Solutions

PRL Site Solutions Ltd is a labour supply, site services, and construction recruitment company based in Merseyside. We operate across the UK providing services to clients in the construction, infrastructure, and industrial sectors.

Our workforce includes directly employed staff, contractors, and agency labour. We engage a supply chain of approximately 300 suppliers and subcontractors across the UK.

Our Supply Chains

Our supply chain includes:

  • Labour supply and staffing agencies
  • Plant hire and equipment suppliers
  • Materials and consumables suppliers
  • Specialist subcontractors

We recognise that the construction, facilities management, and labour supply sectors carry elevated risk of modern slavery and forced labour, particularly within sub-contracted and agency workforces. We take this seriously and apply appropriate scrutiny at supplier onboarding and ongoing review.

Policies in Relation to Slavery and Human Trafficking

We maintain the following internal policies relevant to modern slavery:

  • Anti-Slavery and Human Trafficking Policy — sets out our zero-tolerance position and the responsibilities of staff and management
  • Recruitment and Right to Work Policy — ensures we verify the identity and legal right to work of all individuals engaged by PRL
  • Whistleblowing Policy — provides a confidential route for staff or suppliers to raise concerns without fear of reprisal
  • Supplier Code of Conduct — sets minimum ethical standards expected of all supply chain partners

Copies of these policies are available on request from our management team.

Due Diligence Processes

To identify and address risk, PRL Site Solutions takes the following steps:

  • Conducting supplier checks and pre-qualification assessments prior to engagement
  • Requiring all labour supply agencies to confirm compliance with the Modern Slavery Act and Employment Agencies Act
  • Verifying right-to-work documentation for all directly engaged workers
  • Including anti-slavery obligations in supplier and subcontractor contracts
  • Reviewing agency payroll arrangements to ensure workers are paid correctly and not subject to excessive deductions
  • Maintaining visibility of accommodation arrangements where PRL is involved in housing arrangements for workers

Risk Assessment

We have assessed our highest risk areas as:

Elevated Risk Areas

  • Agency and subcontracted labour, particularly on large or multi-site projects
  • Supply chains involving overseas sourcing
  • Cash-in-hand or informal labour arrangements

We actively work to identify and eliminate any such arrangements from our operations.

Training and Awareness

PRL Site Solutions ensures that relevant staff are aware of the risks of modern slavery and understand their responsibilities. This includes:

  • Awareness briefings for managers and supervisors responsible for workforce engagement
  • Guidance on identifying warning signs of exploitation in the workforce
  • Clear reporting lines for raising concerns internally or with relevant authorities

Key Performance Indicators

We measure our performance in this area through:

  • Number of supplier compliance checks completed
  • Staff awareness and training completion rates
  • Number of concerns raised and actions taken
  • Annual review of this statement and associated policies

Reporting a Concern

If you believe you have witnessed signs of modern slavery or human trafficking — within our workforce, supply chain, or operations — we ask you to report it immediately.
