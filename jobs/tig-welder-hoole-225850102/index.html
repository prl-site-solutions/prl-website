The role

TIG Welder

The Role:

We are looking for an experienced TIG Welder to join our team on a busy civil / industrial site.

You will be welding pipework and structural steel to a high standard, working to drawings and specs.

Duties:TIG welding on stainless steel, carbon steel and aluminium - pipework, plate and structural

Reading and working from engineering drawings, weld maps and WPSPreparing materials - cutting, grinding, bevelling, tacking and fit-up

Carrying out root runs, fill and cap to required standard

Visual inspection of own welds - rectifying defects

Working alongside pipefitters and platers

Following site RAMS, permit to work and hot works proceduresKeeping work area tidy and safe

Requirements:Proven TIG welding experience - 3+ years minimum

Valid coded welding certs - e.g. BS EN ISO 9606-1 / ASME IX - 6G pipe would be a big plus

CSCS card - Blue Skilled Worker preferred

Own PPE and welding mask / tools

Able to pass a weld test on site

Right to Work in UK

Desirable:MIG / MMA experienceExperience on pipework, pressure pipe, hygienic / sanitary tubeIPAF / PASMA / Confined Space

CIS weekly payments only
