Questions contractors ask

What types of workers does PRL Site Solutions supply?

CSCS-certified skilled tradesmen, general labourers, groundworkers, plant operators (CPCS/NPORS), site managers, supervisors and project coordinators. We also cover energy-from-waste, M&E and industrial construction.

How quickly can PRL supply workers to a site?

The office is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Mobilisation is faster than that: pre-checked people can be on site the next day in most cases, across the Northwest and the rest of the UK.

Are all PRL contractors CSCS certified?

Yes. Operatives hold a valid CSCS card, or the right scheme card for the trade. Plant operators carry CPCS or NPORS. Right to work, tickets and IR35 are checked in PRISM before anyone starts.

What areas does PRL Site Solutions cover?

Registered in Prenton, Wirral, and working across the Northwest: Wirral, Liverpool, Manchester, Chester and Cheshire and Warrington, plus sites in Wales, the Midlands and southern England.

How do I register as a contractor?

Apply at prismworkforce.online/apply, or call 0800 772 3959. Then use the PRISM app for timesheets, documents and assignments.

What is the PRISM app?

PRISM is our contractor portal. Timesheets, CSCS and ticket uploads, expiry dates and live assignments, from a phone.
